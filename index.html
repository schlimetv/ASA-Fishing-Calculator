Quick tips

›Always fish Salmon over Coelacanth for a higher loot multiplier.
›Get a rod from Alpha Mosasaur or Alpha Squid kills; rod quality is the biggest lever.
›Can't get a big fish to bite? Kill the small fish around it so it is the only target.
›Fish from a chair or bench. Place either on a raft to make a mobile fishing platform.
›Rods can't be repaired, so bring spares for a long session.
›Watch for weekend loot rate events; they stack on top of fish quality.
